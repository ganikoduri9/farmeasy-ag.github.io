About FarmEasy

Rooted in agriculture.
Growing across industry.

FarmEasy builds practical IoT systems for agriculture and is extending that work into industrial monitoring and automation. We bring sensors, remote telemetry, alerts and controls together around the needs of each site.

Our purpose

Help people stay connected to the conditions that matter to their work, whether they are managing irrigation in a field or monitoring a tank in a factory.

How our work connects

Our agricultural work includes soil moisture sensing, irrigation control and solar-powered remote telemetry. Industrial projects extend that foundation to tank levels, process temperatures and radar sensors. We also evaluate additional sensor types, including dissolved oxygen.

We’re also planning personalized AI assistance inside the FarmEasy portal, so teams can ask site-specific questions about connected readings, alerts and reports.

The application changes. The starting point stays practical: understand the measurement, connect it to a useful view, and define the alerts or actions the site needs.
